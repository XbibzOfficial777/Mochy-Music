# Mochy Xbibz 8.3.3-xbibz.16

<!--lang:id-->

Kode versi: **89**. Android 7 ke atas.

Pengembang: **Xbibz Official777**.

## Yang berubah

- Kartu pemutar di notifikasi dan **layar kunci** mengikuti warna sampul lagu yang sedang diputar (seperti Spotify), termasuk **Android 16**. Pewarnaan memakai MediaStyle + `setColorized` dari sampul; Live Update (promoted ongoing) tidak dipakai karena Google melarang MediaStyle berwarna dipromosikan — itu yang bikin kartu abu-abu.
- Kalau lagu punya lirik tersinkron, **baris yang sedang dinyanyikan** ikut tampil di kartu itu.
- Kanal notifikasi pemutar diganti ke saluran baru yang tetap senyap, supaya Android mengizinkan pewarnaan.

## What changed

- The notification / **lock-screen** media card takes its colour from the current cover art (Spotify-style), including **Android 16**. Colour comes from MediaStyle + `setColorized` on the artwork. Live Updates (promoted ongoing) are not used: Google forbids colourised MediaStyle from being promoted, which is what left the card grey.
- When synced lyrics are available, the **line being sung** is shown on that card.
- The playback notification channel was replaced with a still-silent channel so Android will actually colourise it.
