## Yang berubah

- **Optimasi penggunaan RAM** di Perilaku aplikasi (default mati): cache sampul di memori, buffer pemutar, dan centang widget diperkecil. Tombol kosongkan RAM melepaskan cache gambar Coil dan menampilkan ukurannya.
- **Optimasi panas perangkat** (default mati): matikan refresh tinggi / canvas / bloom ekstra milik aplikasi ini. Status termal dari PowerManager; haptik berhenti jika chip sudah hangat.
- Angka memori dari ActivityManager. Tidak membunuh aplikasi lain, tidak mengklaim mendinginkan seluruh HP.

Version code: **91**. Android 7 and up. **Pre-release (Beta).**

Developer: **Xbibz Official777**.

## What changed

- **Optimize RAM usage** in App behaviour (off by default): smaller in-memory cover cache, player ahead-buffer, and widget ticks. Free app RAM drops Coil's decoded covers and shows the bytes released.
- **Optimize device heat** (off by default): this app drops high refresh, canvas, and extra lyrics bloom. Thermal status comes from PowerManager; haptics stop when the chip is already warm.
- Memory figures come from ActivityManager. Other apps are not killed. The whole phone is not claimed to be cooled.

## Not claimed

Official Mochy issues that need a physical device or YouTube's servers are not patched here: #229, #243, #245, #249, #216, #191, #256, #228.
